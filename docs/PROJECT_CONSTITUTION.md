# Project Constitution

## Vision

Build the most reliable open-source Institutional Market Intelligence & Decision Platform for halal cryptocurrency markets.

---

## Mission

Transform raw market data into explainable, evidence-based trading decisions.

---

# Engineering Principles

## Rule 001 — Single Responsibility

Every module must have one clear responsibility.

---

## Rule 002 — Explainability

No decision may be generated unless it can later be explained.

---

## Rule 003 — Evidence First

Indicators are evidence, not decisions.

---

## Rule 004 — Stable Migration

Legacy logic is migrated incrementally.
Never rewrite validated behaviour unnecessarily.

---

## Rule 005 — Deterministic Trades

Trade targets remain fixed after activation unless a lifecycle event explicitly changes them.

---

## Rule 006 — Test Before Replace

Every V2 component must be tested before replacing legacy code.

---

## Rule 007 — Configuration Driven

Parameters belong in configuration, not hard-coded constants.

---

## Rule 008 — Auditability

Every important decision must leave an audit trail.
